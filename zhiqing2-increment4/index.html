Welcome to the MonoMuse Museum

MonoMuse is a contemporary museum dedicated to exploring the intersection of art, technology, and human creativity. Through immersive exhibitions, interactive installations, and rotating collections, MonoMuse invites visitors to discover how digital innovation is transforming the way we create, experience, and understand art. Whether you are a first-time visitor or a returning member, our exhibitions offer new perspectives on the role of technology in culture and everyday life. Plan your visit to experience cutting-edge exhibitions, hands-on experiences, and events designed to inspire curiosity and creativity.

MonoMuse is more than a museum — it is a living, breathing space where art and technology converge to spark new ways of thinking.

From our founding in 2016, MonoMuse has been committed to making contemporary art accessible to everyone. Our rotating exhibitions feature works by emerging and established artists from around the world, exploring themes of identity, innovation, and the human experience. Beyond exhibitions, MonoMuse offers workshops, artist talks, educational programs, and community events designed to bring people together through creativity. Whether you spend an hour or an entire day with us, there is always something new to discover at MonoMuse.
